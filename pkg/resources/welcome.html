GlobeSwitch

Instant keyboard layout switching on a single press of the 🌐 (Fn) key. A menu-bar app, no windows.

This installer puts GlobeSwitch.app into your Applications folder and launches it.

Right after the installation:

  1. macOS asks for the Accessibility permission — click “Open System Settings” and enable GlobeSwitch. Without it the app cannot see the 🌐 key.
  2. GlobeSwitch asks whether to turn off the system's own 🌐 key action — click Turn Off.
  3. Optionally enable Launch at login in the app's menu (the 🌐 icon in the menu bar).

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer. Apple Silicon and Intel.
